# How To Trade

*A Complete Guide to Futures, Markets and What Moves Them.* Made by kw.

A free trading textbook that reads like a real book: open it, turn the pages, tap any underlined term for its
definition. **Read it here: https://kdubsk1.github.io/how-to-trade/** (best on a computer; works on phones).

**Work in progress.** The book grows chapter by chapter; chapters not yet written show what they will cover.
Chapters the author has not yet reviewed say "not yet reviewed by kw".

**To read it offline:** download this repository as a ZIP (green "Code" button, "Download ZIP"), unzip it, and
double-click `index.html`.
